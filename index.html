app.favi.finance · Effective 17 October 2026

In short: FΛVI (FAVI) has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your accounts, balances and transactions are stored only on your phone, encrypted. The developer does not receive any of your data.

1. Who we are

FΛVI (also written FAVI, Android package app.favi.finance) is a personal finance tracker for Android that shows your bank accounts, cash, crypto wallets, stocks, debts and goals in one place. It is developed and maintained by Max Maersk, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (account names, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring payments, goals, debts, wallet addresses, settings) is stored only on your device.

  • Data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Access is protected by a PIN code; fingerprint unlock is optional.
  • Android cloud backup of the app's data is turned off, and screenshots of the app are blocked.
  • We operate no servers and have no copy of your data.

3. Backup and sync (optional)

If you export a backup or turn on sync, the app writes one file, encrypted with a password you set, to a location you choose with the Android file picker (for example your phone storage or a cloud drive such as Google Drive). We never see this file or the password. The storage provider you choose handles the file under its own privacy policy.

4. Internet requests to third-party services

To show exchange rates, prices and wallet balances, the app sends requests directly from your phone to public services. These requests contain only:

  • currency, coin and stock codes (for example EUR, BTC, AAPL);
  • the public wallet addresses you add, to read their balances;
  • the website domain of a bank you added (for example mbank.pl), only if you turn on bank icons in Settings (off by default), to download its icon.

Account names, amounts and transactions are never sent. Seed phrases and private keys are never requested. Like any internet request, these services receive your IP address and basic technical information and process it under their own privacy policies.

5. Notifications and widget

Reminders and price alerts are created on your device; nothing is sent to us. Their content is hidden on the lock screen. The home screen widget shows an amount only if you turn this on in Settings.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: for the requests described in section 4.
  • Biometrics: optional fingerprint unlock. Fingerprint data stays in Android and is never available to the app.
  • Notifications: for reminders and price alerts, only if you turn them on.

7. What we do not do

  • No sign-up or login, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No connection to your bank, and no payments or transfers of money or crypto. The app only tracks and displays your finances.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it.

8. Deleting your data

Delete the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything from your phone. You can also set the app to erase all data after several wrong PIN attempts. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

9. Children
